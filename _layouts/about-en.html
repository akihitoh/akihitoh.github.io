Akihito Hiromori

廣森 聡仁  ·  Ph.D. in Engineering
Associate Professor, D3 Center, The University of Osaka
Associate Professor (concurrent), Dept. of Information Networking, Graduate School of Information Science and Technology

I design and evaluate information systems that account for human mobility, combining real-world sensing of people, vehicles and environments with simulation, optimization and machine learning. Current topics include neural surrogate models for simulation, smart-city optimization, 3D point-cloud sensing and disaster-resilient networking.
